About KMER ROOM

KMER ROOM is Cameroon's leading platform connecting tenants with property owners.

Founded in 2020, we've helped thousands of students, professionals, and families find their perfect living spaces in Yaoundé and beyond. Our mission is to simplify the process of finding rooms and listing properties through our user-friendly platform.

Whether you're a student looking for affordable accommodation near your university or a property owner wanting to reach qualified tenants, KMER ROOM provides the tools and support you need.

Why Choose Us?

  • Verified property listings
  • Direct communication with landlords/tenants
  • No hidden fees or commissions
  • 24/7 customer support
  • Secure payment options
