How Our Platform Works

Streamlining the rental process from tenant search to signed agreement and beyond

For Tenants

1

Create Profile

Sign up and create your detailed tenant profile. Add references, rental history, and verification documents to build trust with landlords.

Our verification system helps establish your credibility before you even start your search.

2

Search & Filter

Use our advanced search tools to find properties that match your criteria - location, price, amenities, and more.

Save your favorite listings and get alerts when similar properties become available.

3

Book Viewings

Schedule viewings directly through our platform. Choose available time slots that work for you.

Receive automatic reminders and directions to make the process seamless.

4

Apply & Sign

Submit digital applications directly to landlords. All your documents are stored securely for easy access.

Once approved, sign your rental agreement electronically and pay your security deposit through our secure payment system.

For Landlords

1

List Your Property

Create detailed listings with photos, amenities, and pricing. Our platform guides you through the process to create compelling listings.

Set your own criteria for tenants and rental terms.

2

Screen Tenants

Review tenant applications with verified profiles, credit checks, and rental history all in one place.

Our rating system helps you identify quality tenants quickly.

3

Manage Bookings

Use our calendar system to manage property viewings efficiently. Automate reminders and follow-ups.

Track all inquiries and applications in one organized dashboard.

4

Digital Agreements

Generate legally compliant rental agreements with customizable terms.

Enable electronic signatures for fast, paperless processing. Store all documents securely in the cloud.
